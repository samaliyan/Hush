# Changelog

## 1.0

- Removes advertising texts as soon as they arrive, while Google Messages stays your messaging app.
- Finds advertising by the unsubscribe code (لغو۱۱), advertising lines (1000, 2000, 3000, 5000) and your own lists.
- Login codes, bank texts and texts from contacts are kept.
- Clean up old texts in one go, after checking the list.
- Removed texts are kept for 7 days and can be restored.
- Optional: hide the notification of a removed text, and play the sound only for real texts.
- Checks GitHub for new versions when opened.
